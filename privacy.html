Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Jarvis is a private personal assistant. Its only user is its owner; it is not available to anyone else.

What data Jarvis accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, Jarvis reads the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts and Google Drive data, and may create calendar events and email drafts at the owner's request.

How the data is used

The data is used only to organise the owner's tasks, deadlines, reminders and daily briefings. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Data is stored encrypted, in transit and at rest, in a private database accessible only to the owner. Raw notifications and messages are deleted after 30 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
